# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Report violations by opening a private security advisory on this repository or emailing security@sperax.io. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation.
